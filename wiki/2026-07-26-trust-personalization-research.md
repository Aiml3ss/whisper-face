# Trust & Personalization Research Notes

## Summary

Seventeen modules that share one house style: fail closed with broken
indistinguishable from absent; content-free self-validating receipts;
bounded evidence in both directions; layered authority
(evidence ≠ eligibility ≠ activation); and shadow-first measurement
before anything acts.

## Key Takeaways

- Four features ship off and unlock only via 0600 physical-evidence
  receipts with policy, model, and evidence pinning.
- New contradicting evidence demotes already-promoted priors; reloads
  re-shadow everything.
- Filesystem evidence is structurally forbidden from claiming model
  readiness (a constructor assertion).
- Several modules are deliberately not yet wired
  (compatibility fingerprint, proof recovery, wallet execution) and the
  docs say so plainly.
