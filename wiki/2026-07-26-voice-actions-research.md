# Voice-Action Foundations Research Notes

## Summary

The action layer's defining shape: pure decisions separated from
narrow single-verb boundaries, explicit human gestures between them,
single-use nonces popped under locks, frozen-evidence rechecks, and
content-free receipts on every failure path.

## Key Takeaways

- The compose adapter binds no send API; opening the sheet is the
  entire capability.
- Text fields are captured but excluded from press-safe roles by
  construction.
- Drop-to-target has no transaction module at all — nothing can start a
  drag.
- macOS offers no atomic compare-and-swap or compare-and-clear; the two
  affected paths document their microscopic residual races honestly.
