# Support and setup pilot

Whisper Face is testing whether voluntary support and a limited Mac setup
service could sustain the project. This is an interest pilot only: no payment
account is connected, money is not currently accepted, and no Supporter status
or concierge service is being sold.

## The free-local covenant

The community product keeps straightforward local dictation available with:

- no account or word cap;
- local ASR and cleanup, with no required speech cloud;
- the one-click Mac and Windows installers;
- the complete core accuracy, privacy, accessibility, correction, export,
  deletion, and recovery experience; and
- public source under the repository's community license.

Those capabilities are never paywalled. This is a product commitment, not an
additional software license or warranty.

Whisper Face will not sell speech, transcripts, correction history, or
behavioral profiles; add advertising or tracking to local dictation; force a
cloud account; or make privacy and accessibility paid upgrades.

## Proposed voluntary Supporter recognition

If the owner later activates a payment channel, proposed benefits are limited
to non-core recognition and public project communication:

- an optional public thank-you or Supporter badge; and
- a concise public roadmap/update digest.

Support would not buy exclusive builds, product features, higher accuracy,
priority bug fixes, faster security fixes, private roadmap control, or
preferential access to core support. Final benefits, prices, refunds, taxes,
and payment terms require an explicit owner decision before money is accepted.

## Mac concierge interest pilot

The proposed concierge pilot is one user-directed session for one Mac:

- verify the normal one-click install and local health checks;
- help the user select and migrate only the local configuration they choose;
  and
- leave the user with the public verification and recovery instructions.

The user remains at the keyboard. The pilot does not request credentials,
payment-card data, raw audio, transcripts, snippets, private logs, or an upload
of personal configuration. It does not include unattended access, an SLA or
warranty, custom development, ongoing administration, or commercial-license
rights. A maintainer must explicitly accept a request based on capacity before
any scheduling occurs.

Do not include private data in the public issue. The interest form asks only
for a GitHub-visible response, platform, and general goal. If a request is
accepted later, scheduling and any necessary private coordination must move to
an owner-approved channel before the session.

## What the pilot measures

Submissions are stated interest only, not revenue, conversion, or
willingness-to-pay evidence. Reports must:

- count unique non-bot GitHub accounts and deduplicate updates;
- publish a dated count, including zero;
- keep every response nonbinding; and
- distinguish requested, accepted, completed, and declined without recording
  private session contents.

No payment, cloud, scheduling, or customer-management infrastructure should be
built from stated interest alone. Open the
[Supporter and concierge interest form](https://github.com/Aiml3ss/whisper-face/issues/new?template=supporter-concierge-interest.yml)
only if you are comfortable making the response public.

through a signed agreement described in
